## Ideas for SMDA Project

80% of the course assessment is based on a team project. Each team is supposed to deliver a project that is equivalent to the amount of work in a short paper at NLP conferences (4-5 pages + unlimited references and appendices in the [ACL template](https://github.com/acl-org/acl-style-files)). You can find examples of such papers at the end. 

The best projects are on topics that interest you and spark your curiosity, so we strongly encourage you to be as creative as possible when designing a project. Here are some ideas to help you get started:

### Wikipedia

-   Analyzing arguments in Wikipedia talk pages
    
-   Characterizing recommended articles on English Wikipedia’s homepage: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Page](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Main_Page)
    
-   Content differences in articles between English Wikipedia and the articles in other languages [ideal if team members speak the other languages]
    

### Bluesky

-   Comparison of who is included in a set of Bluesky starter packs vs. who is active in the respective communities, also considering rankings
    
-   Does Bluesky’s "cozy" subculture (based on hashtags or keywords) use significantly different linguistic structures (e.g., more first-person, fewer imperatives) than mainstream posts?
    
-   How "biased" are Bluesky community labelers (targeting spam, etc.)?
    
-   Comparison of the top posts from different custom feeds
    

### Youtube

-   Characterizing brainrot content and their audiences (could also be done for Tiktok or both)
    
-   Difference in language use and topics between long form content (>10 mins) and short form videos
    

### Tiktok

-   Science communication on Tiktok: which research papers are mentioned on Tiktok, in which contexts, and with which stance?
    
-   What do politicians share on Tiktok?
    
-   Analysis of the captions of low-effort, AI-generated "content farm" videos on TikTok/YouTube. What are the common rhetorical tricks used to keep viewers engaged?
    

### Multiplatform studies

-   Wikipedia links posted on Reddit: [https://gildersleve.uk/WikiReddit-Dataset-Preprint/](https://gildersleve.uk/WikiReddit-Dataset-Preprint/)
    
-   Sentiment towards Artificial Intelligence and Large Language Models on Reddit vs Bluesky
